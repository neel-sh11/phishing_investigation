
# PH-003 — Payment Redirection Analysis

## 1. Case Information

- Case ID: PH-003
- Scenario: Business Invoice Fraud
- Analysis Type: Payment Instruction Fraud
- Evidence Type: Simulated

## 2. Email References

- Invoice Number: INV-2026-1042
- Claimed Sender: Global Supplies Accounts Department
- Requested Action: Use updated payment instructions
- Deadline: 24 hours

## 3. Suspicious Indicators

1. Unexpected notification of changed banking details.
2. Request to modify supplier payment instructions.
3. Urgency surrounding an outstanding invoice.
4. Sender authentication domains do not align with the visible From domain.

## 4. URL and Attachment Analysis

- URL Present: No
- Attachment Present: No
- URL Reputation Check: Not applicable
- Attachment Analysis: Not applicable

## 5. Business Risk

Potential financial loss through unauthorized payment
redirection to an attacker-controlled account.

## 6. Recommended Verification

1. Do not change supplier bank details based solely on email.
2. Contact the supplier using a previously verified phone number.
3. Confirm the payment change with an authorized representative.
4. Follow the organization's dual-approval payment process.
5. Escalate suspicious requests to the security team.

## 7. Assessment

Suspicious payment-redirection attempt.
Further verification of the supplier identity is required.

All evidence is simulated.
